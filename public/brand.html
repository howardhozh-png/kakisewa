05 — Voice & Copy

Writing rules

kakisewa speaks to professional property agents. Confident, clear, no fluff.

✓ Do
  • Use short, action-first sentences
  • Speak to outcomes: "Earn half a month commission"
  • Use Malaysian context: RM, Bukit Jalil, owner/tenant
  • Sentence case for UI labels and headings
  • Numbers are specific: "60 days", "RM 22,355"
  • Warm but professional tone
✗ Don't
  • Use em dashes (—) or en dashes in copy
  • Use passive voice or corporate jargon
  • ALL CAPS for emphasis (use bold or colour)
  • Exclamation marks in neutral UI text
  • Vague filler: "Please note that...", "In order to..."
  • British/American spelling inconsistency
