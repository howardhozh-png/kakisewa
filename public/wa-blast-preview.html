How WA Auto-Blast works
💻

Keep your laptop awake

Messages send from your laptop in the background. Make sure it stays on, connected to the internet, and not in sleep mode.

📱

Link your WhatsApp (first time only)

On your phone, open WhatsApp and go to Settings → Linked Devices → Link a Device. Scan the QR code shown on your laptop screen.

✅

Stays connected automatically

Once linked, your WhatsApp session is remembered. No need to scan again unless you manually unlink.

⏱

Sends within your time windows

Messages go out automatically during the windows you set below. Outside those hours, the queue pauses and resumes the next day.
