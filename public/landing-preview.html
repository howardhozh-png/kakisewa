Common questions

Is there really no credit card needed?
None. You get 2 full months free. Sign up, add your contracts, and start tracking. If it’s not for you, just stop. No billing, no cancellation process.
How does kakisewa remind me about renewals?
You get push notifications and email alerts 60, 30, and 7 days before each contract expires. You never have to manually check.
Can I import my existing contracts?
Yes. Upload a CSV or Excel file and kakisewa maps your columns automatically. Most agents have all their data in within 10 minutes.
Do I need to be tech-savvy?
Not at all. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use kakisewa. Designed for agents always on their phone, not sitting at a desk.
What happens after the 2-month trial?
You choose a plan that fits how many properties you manage. Plans start at RM 30/month. Cancel anytime, your data is always yours to export.
